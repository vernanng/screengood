ScreenGood — how to open it the first time
==========================================

1. Drag ScreenGood.app onto the Applications link in this window.

2. Double-click ScreenGood in /Applications.
   macOS will say Apple could not verify ScreenGood.app.
   Click Done. Do not click Move to Trash.

   This is expected. The GitHub build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
   It does not mean malware was found.

3. Open Settings -> Privacy & Security.
   Scroll to the Security section near the bottom.

4. Next to the message saying ScreenGood was blocked, click Open Anyway.
   Confirm Open Anyway again and authenticate if asked.

ScreenGood launches. Later double-clicks open normally.

Terminal alternative (after copying the app to /Applications):

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ScreenGood.app
  open /Applications/ScreenGood.app

Next, grant Accessibility and Input Monitoring once when the app asks.
See the project README: https://github.com/vernanng/screengood
